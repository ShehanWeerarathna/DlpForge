The first public release of DlpForge, a free Windows app that puts a friendly interface on top of
[yt-dlp](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp). Paste a link, pick video or audio and a quality, and download.

## Install

1. Download **DlpForge-0.2.0-win-x64.zip** below and unzip it anywhere.
2. Run **DlpForge.exe**. You don't need to install .NET.
3. On first start, click **Install tools** on the banner (or open **Settings > Tools**) to download yt-dlp,
   FFmpeg and Deno.

Requires Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit).

The exe isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click
**More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**Portable mode:** put an empty file named `portable.txt` next to `DlpForge.exe` to keep settings, history
and tools in a `data` folder beside the exe instead of `%LOCALAPPDATA%\DlpForge`.

## What's in it

- **Paste, drop or search**: a link bar that is always visible. Paste links, drop `.url` shortcuts or `.txt`
  lists, or type words to search YouTube. **Quick download** (Shift+Enter) skips the card and reuses your last
  settings.
- **Pick the format**: video or audio, quality presets from 2160p to 360p, container or audio format, or an
  exact-format table.
- **Playlists**: every item listed with checkboxes, plus select all, none or invert.
- **Download queue**: 1 to 10 downloads at once with live progress, speed and ETA. Pause, resume, cancel and
  retry. The queue survives restarts.
- **Cut and split**: download only parts of a video, picked on a timeline or by chapter, or save one file per
  chapter. Remove or mark sponsor segments with SponsorBlock.
- **Embed extras**: thumbnail, metadata, chapters and subtitles. Edit the title and artist before downloading.
- **Schedule and tray**: start downloads at a set time. Tray mode keeps DlpForge running when the window is
  closed.
- **Cookies**: use cookies from your browser or a cookies.txt file for videos that need you to be signed in.
  **YouTube account** lets you sign in once in DlpForge's own window and refresh YouTube cookies later
  without typing your password. **Verify** checks whether a cookies.txt file still signs you in.
- **Power tools**: saved command templates, extra yt-dlp arguments and a Terminal page that runs yt-dlp
  directly.
- **History and logs**: searchable, sortable history with thumbnails, and a log for every download.
- **Tools managed for you**: install and update yt-dlp, FFmpeg and Deno, switch yt-dlp channels and roll back
  a version.
- **Fits Windows**: notifications with Open and Show in folder, taskbar progress, and light and dark themes.

## Good to know

- The YouTube account window needs the Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime. It comes with Windows 11 and most
  Windows 10 PCs; if it's missing, get it from [Microsoft](https://developer.microsoft.com/microsoft-edge/webview2/).
- DlpForge doesn't update itself yet. Watch this repository's releases for new versions.
